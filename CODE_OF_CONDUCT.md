# Code of conduct

We want participation in this project to be respectful, safe, and constructive.
That expectation applies in issues, pull requests, reviews, discussions, and
other project spaces.

## Expected behavior

- Treat people with respect and assume good faith while discussing the work.
- Give specific, technical feedback without personal attacks or harassment.
- Respect privacy. Never post subject data, credentials, or private
  correspondence to demonstrate a problem.
- Use the project only for lawful, authorized research and do not ask
  contributors to help target, intimidate, or expose a person.
- Accept maintainer decisions about safety, scope, source policy, and moderation.

## Unacceptable behavior

Harassment, threats, discrimination, sexualized attention, doxxing, deliberate
privacy violations, and sustained disruption are not accepted. Publishing
another person's private information or investigation evidence is a serious
violation.

## Enforcement

Report conduct concerns privately through the repository's Security Advisories
when confidentiality is needed. Maintainers may edit or remove content, close or
lock threads, reject contributions, or temporarily or permanently restrict
participation. Reports will be reviewed in context and handled as privately as
practical.
